GenericAgent is a high-performance, self-evolving AI desktop application designed to act as a powerful autonomous assistant. It combines a modern web-based frontend with a robust Rust/Tauri shell and a flexible Python backend to provide deep system integration and advanced AI capabilities.

- **Self-Evolving Core**: Ability to adapt and refine its own operational procedures.
- **Multi-LLM Orchestration**: Support for various state-of-the-art Large Language Models.
- **Voice Interaction**: Integrated voice-to-text and text-to-speech for natural interaction.
- **Desktop Integration**: Native OS capabilities powered by Tauri and Rust.
- **Autonomous Task Execution**: Capable of planning and executing complex multi-step workflows.
- **Extensible Plugin System**: Easily add new skills and tools to the agent's repertoire.
